Connect to your assigned devices, then choose your interface. Start in the browser, build with the REST API, or connect an AI agent.

## What would you like to do?

- [Use a device in the browser](https://phoneuse.com/console) — Have an invitation? Open your workspace and view your assigned devices. No installation required.
- [Build with the REST API](/quickstart?path=api) — Make your first request, inspect a frame, and build your own workflow.
- [Connect an AI agent](/mcp) — Set up the MCP bridge for your agent host and discover device tools.

Setting up hardware for your customers? Follow the [station setup guide](/station-setup), then [assign customer access](/customer-access).

## Your code. Real-world execution.

PhoneUse makes physical devices available through a browser console, a REST API, and an MCP bridge. You observe an HD mirror or camera frame, select an action, and receive a durable receipt describing the result. Your application owns the workflow; the station owns physical execution.

```text
Your app / browser / agent
         ↓ HTTPS + customer key
PhoneUse station gateway
         ↓ device access + action validation
Control plane → camera + robot arm → real phone
```

## Understand what is available

Access is provisioned by a station operator. The current runtime supports device-scoped customer keys and monthly access grants. Device capabilities are reported live; a listed action may be unavailable until the hardware is ready.

> Enterprise accounts, SSO, organization roles, self-service billing, and cross-station scheduling are not part of this release. Evaluate the shipped interfaces and agree any procurement requirements with your operator.
